De Moivre root laboratory

Solve z⁵ = 32i

First solve; then make multiplication visible.

Solve modulus and argument on the right; read the complete set of five roots on the left.

Teacher cue

On step 02, press “Multiply by w₀” five times. The arrow stays large while the numbered coordinate scale contracts, so students can read both the 18° turn and the doubled true length. On step 03, click five more times: five copies leave 1 together, separate under powers 1–4, then converge at 32i under power 5.

Keyboard: arrows change step, space plays or pauses motion, R resets.
