STATVERSE FIELD GUIDE

From a population to an estimate.

Population · 总体
The fixed probability model for every possible card draw.
Sample · 样本
n independent draws with replacement. The same card may appear again.
Random variable · 随机变量
Xᵢ is the power of card i before drawing; xᵢ is its observed value.
Statistic · 统计量
A function of sample data with no unknown parameters.
Estimator · 估计量
A statistic used as a rule to estimate a population parameter, before seeing the random sample.
Estimate · 估计值
The numerical result of applying the rule to your observed sample.
Sampling distribution · 抽样分布
The probability distribution of a statistic over all possible samples of the same size from the same model.
Standard error · 标准误
The standard deviation of the estimator's sampling distribution; it depends on n, not the number R of simulation repetitions.

18-minute Teacher route · estimator / estimate / sampling distribution

A teacher script, not evidence of a classroom trial. Choose Sampling & estimators, Aurora, mean rule, n3, seed aurora-2026. In Teacher stage3 set/reset the seed, then return to stage2.

  1. 0–2min · Predict. Stage2: identify the fixed pool and independent replacement draws. Must each group have the pool mean?
  2. 2–5min · Experiment. Stage3: hide worked answers; draw one group. Ask students to calculate from the three raw powers before revealing the estimate.
  3. 5–8min · Observe. Next stage reveals the distribution. Trace one entire group to its saved dot, draw again, then recall sample1. Which object changed: parameter, rule or estimate?
  4. 8–11min · Repeat. Add200 samples. n is cards per group; R is complete groups. Bars show relative frequency; dots below are original groups and their vertical positions are not probabilities.
  5. 11–14min · Explain. Change to n2 and inspect25 weighted ordered cases. Compare the probabilities of the lowest-lowest and rarest-rarest pairs; do not treat them as equally likely.
  6. 14–16min · Transfer. Compare fresh n3 and n12 cohorts. Predict centre and SE before changing n; R only improves the simulated picture.
  7. 16–18min · Exit check. Ask each learner to identify parameter, estimator, observed estimate, what one dot represents, and why more R does not improve one future sample.

Real student understanding, timing and back-row projection remain unmeasured. Use the stated IID finite-variance model; the skewed card mean is not exactly Normal at every n.

Additional delivered routes: S1/S3 descriptive correlation and a sourced critical-table subset; S3 one/two-independent-mean z methods; S2 discrete tests; partial S3 χ² fit/independence.
