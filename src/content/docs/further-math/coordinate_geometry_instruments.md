This collection supplements board teaching with constructions that are
difficult to reproduce as static diagrams: moving loci, secants approaching
tangents, focus-directrix ratios, eccentricity, and sections of a double cone.

The instruments are designed for whole-class projection. Each one builds the
mathematics in stages, can be paused or replayed, and settles into a diagram
that remains useful for discussion.

**[Open the full Coordinate Geometry collection →](/interactive-fm/coordinate-geometry/)**

## The nine instruments

1. **Parabola: Focus and Directrix** — construct the equal-distance locus.
2. **A Parameter Draws a Curve** — connect a changing parameter to a moving point and its trace.
3. **Secant to Tangent** — let a secant line converge to the tangent.
4. **Two Foci, Two Conics** — compare constant-sum and constant-difference loci.
5. **Reading Standard Form** — connect equation parameters to visible geometry.
6. **Curve Language Challenge** — predict a curve from its algebraic description.
7. **Eccentricity Unifies Conics** — vary the focus-directrix ratio through ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola.
8. **Slicing a Double Cone** — rotate a spatial model and reveal the Dandelin-sphere argument.
9. **Conic Tangents and Normals** — connect secant limits, gradients, tangents, and normals.

All formula rendering and assets are stored with the collection, so the tools
do not depend on a third-party script or account.
